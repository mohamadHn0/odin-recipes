Lasagna Recipe

Home

Lasagna is a very tradional classic plate, made of pasta , creamy sauce and cheese

    Ingredients:
  • 1 pound sweet Italian sausage
  • 3/4 pound lean ground beef
  • 1/2 cup minced onion
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 (28 ounce) can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 (6 ounce) cans tomato paste
  • 2 (6.5 ounce) cans canned tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 2 tablespoons white sugar
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons dried basil leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon fennel seeds
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 4 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 16 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 pound mozzarella cheese, sliced
  • 3/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
    Instructions:
  1. Cook the sausage and ground beef in a large pot until browned.
  2. Add the onion and garlic, and cook until the onion is translucent.
  3. Stir in the tomatoes, tomato paste, tomato sauce, water, sugar, basil, fennel seeds, Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper.
  4. Simmer the sauce for at least 30 minutes.
  5. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  6. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  7. In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese with the egg and salt.
  8. Assemble the lasagna in a baking dish, layering the noodles, meat sauce, and cheese mixture.
  9. Sprinkle the mozzarella and Parmesan cheeses on top.
  10. Bake for 30 minutes or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
