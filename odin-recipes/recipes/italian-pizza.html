Italian Pizza Recipe

Home

Italian Pizza is a very traditional dish, made with a thin crust, tomato sauce, and various toppings

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup warm water
  • 2 teaspoons active dry yeast
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 cup sliced pepperoni

How to make:

  1. Activate the yeast in warm water with sugar.
  2. Mix the flour, salt, and olive oil to create a dough.
  3. Knead the dough until it's smooth and elastic.
  4. Let the dough rise in a warm place for about an hour.
  5. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  6. Roll out the dough and place it on a baking sheet.
  7. Spread the tomato sauce over the dough.
  8. Sprinkle the mozzarella and Parmesan cheeses on top.
  9. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown.
